It's two fourteen A.M. You've been driving for eleven hours. One more ride, and you're done.

The pickup is outside a bank. Three men in suits. One heavy black bag.

They don't put it in the trunk. They keep it on their laps.

The guy behind you says one thing: "Just drive. Don't look back."

You pull out. Two blocks later, you hear it. Sirens. Far behind you... but getting closer.

You check the mirror. The big man in the middle opens the bag. Cash. Stacks of it.

He drops five bricks on your passenger seat. "That's fifty thousand. Keep driving, and it's yours."

Fifty thousand dollars. That's every bill you owe. That's three years of rent.

The sirens are louder now. Red and blue fill your mirror.

Your phone buzzes. Rider changed destination. The new stop: an empty parking lot by the river.

Then you see it. The man behind you leans forward, and his jacket falls open. Clipped to his belt... is a police badge.

A dirty cop? Or a setup?

If you stop now... who scares you more? The cops behind you... or the cop inside your car?

The light turns green. The sirens are one block away.

Drive... or stop?
